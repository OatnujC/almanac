Minor occasion · Monday 9 November 2026

Amavasya (new moon day, Diwali week)

Amavasya is the Hindu new moon day, observed every lunar month. In 2026 the Amavasya that holds Diwali begins at 11:27 AM on Sunday 8 November and ends at 12:31 PM on Monday 9 November in New Delhi. Because it is in force at Monday’s sunrise, many calendars list Monday as Amavasya too, and some call it Somvati Amavasya, a new moon falling on a Monday, a day for bathing and charity.

That overlap is why Diwali week has a gap this year: Govardhan Puja moves to Tuesday 10 November and Bhai Dooj to Wednesday 11 November in India. Timings vary by city and local panchang (Hindu almanac), and some sources place Govardhan Puja a day earlier outside India, so check your own community’s calendar.
