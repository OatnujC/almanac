Independence Day (Cambodia)

Cambodia observes Independence Day every 9 November, marking its declaration of independence from France on 9 November 1953. France had controlled the country since 1863. King Norodom Sihanouk began pressing for independence in 1949, and in 1953 he succeeded, which is why Cambodians remember him as the father of independence.

The main celebrations are held in Phnom Penh around the Independence Monument, with smaller events in the provinces.
