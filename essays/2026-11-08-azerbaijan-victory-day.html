Victory Day in Azerbaijan — a national holiday from a recent war

8 November is Victory Day in Azerbaijan, a public holiday and a day off work. It commemorates the recapture of the city of Shusha on 8 November 2020, during the 44-day war with Armenia that began on 27 September that year. President Ilham Aliyev declared the holiday by an order dated 3 December 2020, citing the historic significance of that day in the campaign.

This is Azerbaijan’s official remembrance of that war. The fighting, and the wider conflict over Nagorno-Karabakh, remain deeply contested, and the same dates carry a very different meaning for Armenians and for others who lived through the loss, displacement and violence of 2020. Recording the holiday is a way of noticing how a recent war becomes a national anniversary, not of treating one official account as the whole story.
